2. Information other players can see

Your public company profile, any linked validators and any guild pledges (listed above) are visible to everyone who plays online. Choose a company name that does not reveal personal information. Your wallet address and game save are never shown to other players by the Game.

3. Cookies and local storage

The Game stores your save, settings and sign-in session in your browser's local storage so you can pick up where you left off. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics.

4. Service providers

We use these providers to run the Game. They process data on our behalf and only as needed to provide their service:

  • Supabase: account sign-in and the game database (servers located in the European Union, Paris region).
  • Vercel: website hosting (United States and worldwide edge network).
  • jsDelivr and Google Fonts: deliver code libraries and fonts to your browser; they receive your IP address when files are downloaded.
  • PulseChain public RPC: the Game asks a public PulseChain node for the latest block number to sync its in-game clock. This request contains no personal information beyond your IP address.
  • PulseChain beacon and explorer APIs (PublicNode, g4mm4, PulseChain Scan): when you link or refresh validators, our server looks up public validator data. Your wallet address is sent to the explorer only when you press "Find mine".
  • WalletConnect (only if offered and you choose it): relays the connection between the Game and your phone wallet.

Because our database is hosted in the EU, your information may be stored and processed outside your country.

5. How we use information

We use information only to run the Game: signing you in, saving progress, showing leaderboards and the world map, running staking and guild features between players, preventing cheating and abuse, and keeping the service secure. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account, save and linked validators until you delete them or ask us to. Public profiles of players inactive for 7 days disappear from the world map but remain in the database until deleted. Guest accounts may become inaccessible if you clear your browser data; connect a wallet in the Company tab to avoid this.

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA. Residents of other U.S. states with privacy laws, and of the EU/UK, may make the same requests at the email above.

8. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), row-level access rules in our database so players can only change their own data, and server-side checks on game actions. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password.
